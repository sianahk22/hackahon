## Brand & Style

This design system translates energetic awareness into an experience of quietude, intention, and natural harmony. Inspired by modern editorial aesthetics and thoughtful digital sanctuaries, the interface eschews the aggressive, gamified metrics and alarmist visual charts typical of energy management utilities. Instead, it invites the user into a contemplative, paper-like space where ecological decisions feel gentle, deliberate, and rewarding.

The design movement is a synthesis of Warm Editorial Minimalism and Tactile Calm. It treats time and electricity not as abstract industrial commodities, but as natural tides—rhythms of sun, wind, and river. Visual noise is stripped down completely, replaced by generous breathing room, organic tint shifts, delicate serif typography, and tactile surfaces reminiscent of archival stationery and Japanese book craft.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in botanical depths and unbleached fiber tones. 

- **Primary (`#1B382B`)**: Deep forest pine provides an authoritative, grounding contrast for crucial states, key actions, and dominant headings.
- **Secondary (`#5C7F67`)**: Muted sage acts as a peaceful supporting shade for category signifiers, secondary states, and serene confirmation states.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488`)**: A restrained mineral teal, reserved specifically for dynamic flows of clean power, indicating live generation, river currents, and active ecological windows.
- **Neutral (`#2C3531`)**: Soft charcoal slate replaces harsh pure black, preserving natural warmth and optical softness across body text and hairline dividers.

Surfaces rely entirely on warm paper foundations (`#FAF8F5` and `#F5F2EB`), ensuring an absence of digital glare while lending tactile weight to content cards and floating panels.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial atmosphere that feels closer to an art review or nature journal than a dashboard. 

- **Display & Headings**: Set in **Newsreader**, bringing fluid italic ligatures, humanist grace, and literary poise. Display texts invite users to pause and read metrics like poetry rather than industrial warnings.
- **Body & Functional UI**: Powered by **Inter**, providing absolute clarity, pristine legibility at miniature scales, and a neutral, functional foil to the expressive serif headings.

Numerical timestamps, kilowatts, and wind percentages integrate smoothly within body rhythms, avoiding hyper-stylized monospace fonts in favor of natural optical flow.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a restrained, fluid column system bounded by generous borders of silence. Interfaces are structured around maximum content widths (1120px desktop, 640px reading columns), preventing wide dispersion of energy data and maintaining an intimate, curated viewpoint.

- **Desktop**: A 12-column grid with 24px gutters and spacious 48px outer margins. Content groups float organically like museum plates.
- **Tablet**: An 8-column grid with 20px gutters and 32px margins.
- **Mobile**: A 4-column grid with 16px gutters and 20px margins, shifting primary actions and peaceful recommendations to vertical, card-based stream timelines.

Spacing values (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) govern generous element separation, intentionally offering 1.5x more negative space than conventional utility software to cultivate focus and tranquility.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved primarily through warm tonal layering and soft, low-contrast outlines rather than heavy drop shadows. The design system mimics layers of handmade paper arranged quietly on a wooden writing desk.

- **Flat/Tonal Surfaces**: The root canvas sits on Warm Canvas (`#FAF8F5`). Panels and container modules rest on Paper Surface (`#FFFFFF`) or Secondary Sand (`#F5F2EB`).
- **Low-Contrast Outlines**: Floating cards, sheets, and timeline clusters use whisper-soft borders (`1px solid #EAE5DB`) instead of dark outlines.
- **Ambient Air Shadows**: When an element rises (such as a modal, quick-scheduler popover, or active window card), it casts an ultra-diffused, ambient tone: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(44, 53, 49, 0.03)`. The shadow is tinted with the primary forest hue, never neutral gray or harsh black.

## Shapes

The shape system employs soft, calibrated geometry that removes sharp friction while retaining architectural structure. 

Standard components (cards, text fields, dialogue sheets) utilize `roundedness: 2` (base radius of 8px, scaling to 16px on content cards and 24px on hero moments). Micro-elements such as indicator tags, filter badges, and timeline slots use organic pill caps (`rounded-full`) to simulate smooth river stones, reinforcing nature and time without feeling juvenile or cartoonish.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action**: Solid Forest Pine (`#1B382B`) background with Sand Canvas (`#FAF8F5`) text. Transitions softly on hover to `#234E3D` with subtle scaling (`scale: 0.99` on press).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Unfilled with a hairline boundary (`#EAE5DB`) and Charcoal slate text. On hover, reveals a warm background wash (`#F5F2EB`).
- **Pill Energy Trigger**: Pill-shaped action bar that communicates ideal consumption slots, pairing an subtle pulsing dot in Teal (`#0D9488`) with quiet serif labeling.

### Cards & Time Windows
- **Serene Card**: Crisp white surface resting on sand backdrop, bordered by `1px solid #EAE5DB`. Devoid of harsh separators; visual hierarchy is dictated purely by typography scale and generous internal padding (`space-lg`).
- **Moment Prediction Card**: Features a delicate atmospheric background tint (Sage or Teal wash at 6% opacity) representing peak ecological windows (solar abundance, optimal grid hours). Contains no noisy red warnings; low-efficiency hours are represented through soft, muted charcoal notes.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill geometries styled with unassertive text (`label-sm`).
- Active statuses utilize a warm sage or faint teal tint with zero high-contrast borderlines.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form controls blend seamlessly into cards with light parchment fills (`#FAF8F5`) and subtle bottom boundaries.
- Focus states invoke a gentle mineral teal hairline focus-ring without harsh glow rings.
- Checkboxes and radio selectors employ soft forest green borders and smooth organic fill animations.

### Energy Moment Stream (Custom Component)
- Replaces traditional peak/off-peak bar graphs with an editorial stream of time ribbons. Each ribbon articulates time like a chapter ("Demain, au lever du soleil"), pairing weather conditions, low-carbon percentages, and appliance suitability into elegant prose cards.
